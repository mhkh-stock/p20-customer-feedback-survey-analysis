# P20 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will analyse your customer feedback survey and tell you what to fix first

**What the client gets:** a customer feedback survey analysis for
their retail, clinic, salon or service business — NPS overall, by
branch/location and by channel, average satisfaction overall and by
driver, which driver matters most to satisfaction (Pearson
correlation), a fix-first ranking so they know what to spend on
first, what their detractors actually complain about (counted from
their own comments), the month-by-month NPS trend, and three
prioritised actions. Delivered as a 2-page PDF report plus a live
Excel workbook they can re-run on next month's survey themselves.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | The 2-page PDF report on up to 100 survey responses: NPS overall, by branch and by channel, the fix-first driver, the 3 recommendations |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Everything in Basic + up to 500 survey responses, driver correlations and the full fix-first ranking, detractor themes from their comments, the monthly NPS trend, the live Excel workbook with formulas |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Everything in Standard + up to 2,000 survey responses, the survey questions reviewed and reworded for cleaner answers, a reusable survey-analysis template for their own future exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** collecting the survey responses or chasing
customers to answer (they export their survey from Google Forms,
Typeform or their own system — quoted separately if they need the
survey form built); running the store or retraining staff (that's
their team, not this service — this service shows them what to fix
first); a live dashboard that updates itself (this service is a
report + a workbook they re-run — a live dashboard is a different
gig).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads
their survey export, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — CityMart,
350 survey responses over 6 months, NPS 16.29 (138 promoters,
131 passives, 81 detractors), average satisfaction 4.03/5,
Lahore — Gulberg found at NPS 34.62 against Rawalpindi — Saddar
at 4.05 (a 30.57-point gap), delivery speed found as the fix-first
driver (3.84/5, correlation 0.725 with satisfaction), stock-outs
the top detractor theme (18 of 81 detractors), NPS up 28.67 points
from January to June, 218 automated checks passing, and a 2-page
PDF the buyer sees before ordering.
